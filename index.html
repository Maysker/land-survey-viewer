Shown as delivered

Land Survey Viewer is a viewer, not a CAD, GIS or engineering tool. It never fills gaps with guesses — if something isn't in the drawing, it isn't shown.

Made for one workflow. This preview was developed for a specific user's survey deliverables and working process in Belgium, and it has been tested on a small number of real files. It does not try to be universal or complete. A DWG or DXF is not one fixed format: content, layer conventions, object types and versions differ between surveyors and software. Files that differ from what it was built for may open with missing layers, without terrain, or not at all. Please try it with your own files before relying on it. If something doesn't open as expected, you can open an issue — but never attach survey files you aren't allowed to share.
  • Tested with — Belgian survey drawings (DWG/DXF) in Lambert 72 and the Flemish GM-BT layer convention, plus simple TXT/CSV point lists.
  • Lambert 72 (EPSG:31370) — recognised and placed on the map with imagery.
  • Other coordinate systems — open as a local plan, without imagery or positioning.
  • Flemish GM-BT layers — recognised automatically; other layer names can be classified by hand.
  • Point files — columns detected or mapped by you; comma, semicolon, tab or space; decimal comma supported.
  • No guessing of units, coordinate systems, heights or point codes.
  • No invented terrain — points are never triangulated into a surface.
  • Not read: Civil 3D objects, proxies, solids, meshes, dimensions, images, paper space. They are listed in the import report.
  • No corrections or certification — the surveyor's deliverable remains the reference.

Privacy

Survey drawings often describe someone's property. They stay on your computer.

  • The app's internal server listens on 127.0.0.1 only.
  • Survey files are copied into a local data folder and never uploaded.
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no account.
  • The only network traffic is optional aerial imagery from the official regional services — requested only after you allow it, and only for surveys confirmed to be in Lambert 72.
  • Downloaded imagery is kept locally until you clear it under Privacy.

Data sources. Aerial imagery: Digitaal Vlaanderen (Flanders), Service public de Wallonie (Wallonia), paradigm.brussels / UrbIS (Brussels-Capital Region), under their respective terms. Regional boundaries: SPF Finances / AGDP open data. Third-party software notices ship with the application.
