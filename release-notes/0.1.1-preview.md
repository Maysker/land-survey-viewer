## Changes

- **Building volume uses measured heights only.** If the drawing has no roof heights, or no floor or threshold heights, no volume is shown and the import report says why ("Building volume not built: no roof heights in this drawing." / "…no floor or threshold heights in this drawing."). Previously a 4 m roof or a floor at 0 was assumed.
- **Plan Mode base elevation** of planned buildings is now shown in the drawing's heights, consistent with Inspect and the elevation legend. Previously it was offset by the survey's lowest terrain point.
- **Parcel area** — in Flemish GM-BT drawings the parcel hatch layer is recognised as "Parcel area" (hidden by default) instead of "Not recognised".
- The building volume carries the survey's confirmed coordinate system instead of always Belgian Lambert 72.

## Known limitations

- **Made for one workflow.** This preview was developed for a specific user's survey deliverables and tested on a small set of real files. It is not a universal DWG/DXF viewer; drawings from other surveyors or software may open only partly, or not at all.
- **Not code-signed yet.** Windows SmartScreen may warn on first run: *More info → Run anyway* after verifying the checksum.
- Only Belgian Lambert 72 (EPSG:31370) is placed on the map; other systems open as a local plan.
- The 3D view needs a terrain surface (3DFACE triangles) in the drawing.
- Civil 3D objects, proxies, solids, meshes, dimensions, images and paper space are not read.

## Requirements

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime (installed by the setup if missing), .NET Framework 4.8.
